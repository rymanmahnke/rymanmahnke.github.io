Differential Mounting Assembly

⚠️ Project Problem

  • Baseline - Differential needs to be mounted onto the chassis
  • Old differential mounts did not integrate a jacking point, which required extra manufacturing and design to fix a jacking point onto the car
  • New Front-to-back final gear ratio changes the force and requires a rework of the mounts to ensure stability
  • Not enough validation of hand calculations using technology, which led to point deductions at competitions

🛠️ Project Actions

  • Engineered 3D CAD models in SolidWorks, integrating the jacking point into the differential mounts while still passing all safety requirements
  • Completed topology optimizations of each differential mount in Ansys to eliminate all unnecessary mass.
  • Ran Finite Element Analysis (FEA) of each mount to test stress and strain margins and determine a factor of safety

⏳ In Progress

  • Currently iterating and rerunning FEA simulations and optimize mounts
  • In the process of choosing and calibrating strain gauges
  • Still need to CAM and CNC each part once design is completely finished

Motor Mounting Assembly

⚠️ Project Problem

  • Baseline - Motor Needs to be mounted onto the chassis
  • Old motor mounts were not designed to be weight efficient while still being structurally rigid.
  • New Front-to-back final gear ratio changes the force and requires a rework of the mounts to ensure stability
  • Not enough validation of hand calculations using technology, which led to point deductions at competitions

🛠️ Project Actions

  • Modeled complete 3D assembly geometry in SolidWorks with parameterized mounting interfaces.
  • Ran a topology optimization of geometry in Ansys to ensure all unnecessary mass was eliminated.
  • Conducted Finite Element Analysis (FEA) in Ansys Workbench to identify stress concentrations and yield margins.
  • Incorperated optimal strain gauges with placements based on von Mises stress contours for physical testing validation.

⏳ In Progress

  • Currently iterating and rerunning FEA simulations and optimize mounts
  • In the process of choosing and calibrating strain gauges
  • Still need to CAM and CNC each part once design is completely finished
