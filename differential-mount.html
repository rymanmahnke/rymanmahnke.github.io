Differential Mounting Assembly

Project Goals

  • Design a structural mount capable of supporting torsional load requirements under acceleration.
  • Minimize overall component weight without compromising structural rigidity.
  • Ensure integration with existing chassis attachment points and drivetrain geometry.

Project Actions

  • Modeled 3D geometry in SolidWorks and created parameterized mounting tabs.
  • Conducted Finite Element Analysis (FEA) in Ansys to identify peak stress regions and yield margins.
  • Selected strain gauge locations based on stress contours for physical load testing.

Project Solutions

  • Optimized bracket wall thickness, reducing total weight by 15% while maintaining safety factor thresholds.
  • Implemented reinforced gussets at high-stress mounts to eliminate deflection under dynamic loading.
  • Validated FEA predictions against physical strain gauge data during bench testing.
